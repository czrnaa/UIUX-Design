# Grids & Layout

Grids and layouts are systems used to organize and position elements consistently within an interface.

- **Grid** - A structure of columns, rows, margins, and gutters that helps align content.
- **Layout** - The overall arrangement and relationship of elements on a screen or page.

## For Example

Your desktop frame is: `1440px`

Should content stretch all 1440px? **Usually, no.**

## Some Terms
- **Column** — vertical content region.
- **Gutter** — space between columns.
- **Margin** — outer space around the grid.
- **Container** — area constraining your content.
