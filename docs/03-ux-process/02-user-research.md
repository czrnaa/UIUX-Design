# User Research

**Definition**. User Research is the process of understanding users, their goals, behaviors, frustrations, and needs.

## Why Research?

*Never assume.*
> Evidence > Opinions.

## Research Methods
- Qualitative
  - Interviews
  - Observation
  - Contextual Inquiry
- Quantitative
  - Surveys
  - Analytics
  - Usage Data

## Questions to Ask
- Who are the users?
- What are they trying to do?
- What frustrates them?
- What tools do they currently use?

## Some Usual Deliverables
- Interview Notes
- Survey Results
- Pain Points
- Insights
