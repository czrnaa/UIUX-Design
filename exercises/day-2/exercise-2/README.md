# Exercise 2
Learn how professional products use consistent spacing to create clean, organized, and easy-to-use interfaces.

## Instructions
1. Open the **Spotify** app (desktop, mobile, or web).
2. Navigate to the **Home** screen.
3. Observe the layout and locate:
   - A content card (e.g., an album or playlist)
   - The screen margins
   - Any button (e.g., **Play**, **Follow**, or **Shuffle**)
4. Measure or estimate the spacing values.
5. Record your findings in the table below.

## What to Measure
- `Card Spacing` - Measure the gap **between two adjacent cards** (such as playlists or albums).
- `Screen Padding` - Measure the distance **between the edge of the screen (or content container) and the first piece of content**.
- `Button Padding` - Measure the internal spacing **between the button's text/icon and its edges** (top, bottom, left, and right).

## How to Measure
### Option 1 (Recommended): Use Figma

1. Take a screenshot of the Spotify screen.
2. Drag the screenshot into Figma.
3. Press **Shift + R** to show rulers (optional).
4. Select the **Frame** tool or use guides to align your measurements.
5. Hold **Alt** (Windows) or **Option** (Mac) while hovering between two elements to display the distance between them.
6. Record the spacing shown.

### Option 2: Inspect Spotify Web

1. Open **https://open.spotify.com**.
2. Right-click an element and choose **Inspect**.
3. Use the browser's element inspector.
4. Hover over elements to view the spacing overlay.
5. Look for values related to:
   - `padding`
   - `margin`
   - `gap`

---

> Tip: In Figma, holding Option (Mac) or Alt (Windows) while hovering between two layers is one of the fastest ways to measure spacing. It's a skill you'll use constantly when recreating designs or checking layout consistency.
